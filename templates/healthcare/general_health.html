🧼 Personal Hygiene

Good personal hygiene helps reduce the spread of infections and supports overall wellbeing.

  • Wash hands regularly with soap and clean water.
  • Maintain regular bathing and oral hygiene.
  • Keep clothes and personal belongings clean.
  • Use clean drinking water whenever possible.

🥗 Healthy Diet & Nutrition

A balanced diet provides the body with the nutrients needed for growth, energy and normal body functions.

  • Include a variety of vegetables and fruits.
  • Include protein-rich foods in meals.
  • Drink sufficient safe water.
  • Limit excessive consumption of highly processed foods.

🛡️ Preventive Healthcare

Preventive healthcare focuses on reducing health risks and identifying health problems early.

  • Attend regular health checkups when recommended.
  • Follow recommended vaccination schedules.
  • Maintain good hygiene and sanitation.
  • Seek medical advice when persistent symptoms occur.

💉 Vaccination & Immunization

Vaccines help protect people from certain infectious diseases. Vaccination schedules can vary according to age and health circumstances.

  • Follow the vaccination schedule recommended by healthcare professionals.
  • Keep vaccination records safely.
  • Ask a healthcare professional if you have questions about a vaccine.

🤒 Common Health Problems

Common health complaints can have different causes. Persistent, severe or unusual symptoms should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

  • Fever
  • Cough and cold symptoms
  • Headache
  • Digestive problems
  • Body pain and weakness

🏃 Healthy Lifestyle

Healthy daily habits can support physical and mental wellbeing.

  • Stay physically active according to your ability.
  • Maintain a regular sleep routine.
  • Eat a balanced diet.
  • Avoid tobacco and other harmful substances.
  • Take care of your mental and emotional wellbeing.

🏥 When to Visit a Doctor

Seek professional medical advice when symptoms are severe, persistent, getting worse or causing significant difficulty in daily activities.

  • Severe or worsening symptoms
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe chest pain
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe bleeding
  • Any other medical emergency
Important: This page provides general health awareness information. It does not replace advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.
