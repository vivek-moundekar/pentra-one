🧼 Personal Hygiene & Sanitation

Personal hygiene and sanitation are important for protecting health and reducing the spread of infections. Simple daily habits can help individuals, families and communities maintain a cleaner and healthier environment.

👐 1. Hand Hygiene

Washing hands properly with soap and safe water is one of the most important ways to reduce the spread of germs.

  • Wash hands before preparing or eating food.
  • Wash hands after using the toilet.
  • Wash hands after cleaning a child.
  • Wash hands after coughing, sneezing or blowing the nose.
  • Wash hands after handling waste or dirty materials.
💡 Remember: Use soap and safe water whenever available and clean all parts of the hands properly.

🦷 2. Personal Cleanliness

Regular personal care helps maintain cleanliness and can support overall health.

  • Bathe regularly and keep the body clean.
  • Wear clean clothes when possible.
  • Keep fingernails clean and trimmed.
  • Brush teeth regularly.
  • Keep hair and scalp clean.
  • Keep personal towels and other items clean.

🚽 3. Toilet & Sanitation

Proper sanitation helps prevent contamination of food, water and surroundings.

  • Use a safe and sanitary toilet.
  • Keep toilets clean.
  • Dispose of human waste safely.
  • Wash hands with soap after using the toilet.
  • Avoid open defecation where sanitary toilet facilities are available.

⚠ Important

Poor sanitation can contribute to the spread of water-borne and other infectious diseases.

💧 4. Safe Drinking Water

Safe drinking water is essential for health. Contaminated water can cause infections and illness.

  • Use a safe source of drinking water.
  • Store drinking water in clean containers.
  • Keep water containers covered.
  • Avoid touching drinking water with dirty hands.
  • Follow local guidance for treating unsafe water.
💡 Rural Health Tip: Keep drinking-water storage containers clean and protected from contamination.

🍲 5. Food Hygiene

Good food hygiene helps reduce the risk of food contamination and food-borne illness.

  • Wash hands before preparing food.
  • Keep cooking surfaces and utensils clean.
  • Wash fruits and vegetables properly.
  • Cook food properly.
  • Store cooked food safely.
  • Protect food from insects and other contamination.

🗑️ 6. Waste Management

Proper waste disposal helps keep homes and communities clean and reduces environmental contamination.

  • Dispose of household waste safely.
  • Keep waste away from drinking-water sources.
  • Do not allow waste to accumulate around homes.
  • Keep drains and surrounding areas clean.
  • Follow local waste-management practices.

🦟 7. Preventing Mosquito & Insect Breeding

Standing water can provide breeding places for mosquitoes. Keeping surroundings clean can help reduce mosquito breeding.

  • Do not allow unnecessary stagnant water to collect.
  • Keep water storage containers properly covered.
  • Keep surroundings clean.
  • Follow local public-health advice during outbreaks.

🏠 8. Clean & Healthy Home Environment

A clean home environment supports healthy living for the whole family.

  • Keep living areas clean.
  • Maintain good ventilation where possible.
  • Keep food preparation areas clean.
  • Keep animals and animal waste away from food areas.
  • Keep children away from hazardous waste and contaminated areas.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 9. Hygiene for the Whole Family

Hygiene is everyone's responsibility. Children, adults and elderly people can all benefit from practicing healthy hygiene habits.

  • Teach children proper handwashing.
  • Encourage clean drinking-water practices.
  • Support elderly or disabled family members with hygiene needs.
  • Maintain clean shared spaces.
  • Make hygiene practices part of the family's daily routine.

🩺 10. When to Seek Medical Help

Hygiene and sanitation can reduce many health risks, but they cannot prevent every illness. Seek medical advice when symptoms are persistent or concerning.

  • Persistent fever or illness.
  • Severe or persistent diarrhoea or vomiting.
  • Signs of dehydration.
  • Severe skin infection or worsening wound.
  • Symptoms that are getting worse instead of improving.

🚨 Emergency

For severe breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding or other life-threatening symptoms, seek emergency medical care immediately.

❌ Common Hygiene Myths

Myth: Washing hands with only water is always enough.

Fact: Washing with soap and safe water is recommended when possible.
Myth: Hygiene is only important when someone is sick.

Fact: Regular hygiene practices are important for everyday health and disease prevention.
